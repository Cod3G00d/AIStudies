Acme Tech — Internal FAQ (study document)

Acme Tech was founded in 2019 in São Paulo.
The main product is AcmeFlow, a workflow automation platform.
The Starter plan costs $99/month and includes up to 3 users.
The Pro plan costs $299/month and includes up to 20 users and priority support.
The Pro plan SLA is 99.9% uptime.
Support responds within 4 business hours on Pro and within 24 hours on Starter.
The official AI team stack uses Python, LangChain, LangGraph, and vector databases (Chroma in development, Pinecone in production).
RAG means Retrieval-Augmented Generation: retrieve relevant snippets and use them as context for the LLM.
An agent is a system that uses an LLM to decide which tools to call in a loop until the task is solved.
